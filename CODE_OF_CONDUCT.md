# Contributor Code of Conduct

We want participation in BanglaPDFCheck to be welcoming and respectful. Participants should:

- Be considerate and constructive when discussing ideas and code.
- Critique implementation choices without insulting people.
- Respect privacy and copyright. Do not publish personal documents, private notices, or copyrighted sample PDFs without permission.
- Accept that maintainers may ask for scope reductions or decline changes that are not a fit for the project.

Harassment, discrimination, doxxing, and intentionally disruptive behaviour are not acceptable. A maintainer may edit or remove content that violates these expectations and may block repeat offenders.

To report a conduct concern, open a private contact route through the repository owner rather than posting sensitive details in a public issue.
